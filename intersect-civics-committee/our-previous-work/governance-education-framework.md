# Governance Education framework

Governance only works if people can understand it. The Governance Education Working Group ran from July 2025 to March 2026, under its own [charter](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cnnQ-Xy5f3KqFNJcYh69kH9bA_4fAawDJz_O9nnMbrY/edit?usp=sharing), to make Cardano's governance learnable for DReps, SPOs and community members.

### The framework

The group produced the [Cardano Governance Education Framework](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OdnXvIDGMpWOsk7O1NT2_SDoJZRF9MkA1FwMBARu-0U/edit?usp=sharing), now at version 2, developed through community workshops and expert review.

### Mapping, not duplicating

Rather than write new material and compete with what already existed, the group mapped the landscape. Community-submitted resources were scored against six criteria, accessibility, accuracy, adaptability, inclusivity, relevancy and empowerment, using a published [resource filtering framework](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nlS7Wk6-52Y2d3yWVB-3r5NpbikdILWZ/edit?usp=sharing). Anything averaging 4.0 or above was approved for the knowledge base, with reviewer notes attached. The [resource database](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1--LJDJG0uL3gC0S_BJMezso1fBfVAw4nzlyvY3lafrc/edit?usp=sharing) records every assessment.

The database is maintained openly, with contribution recipes, so the community can keep adding to it rather than letting it decay once the working group closed (26 March 2026).
