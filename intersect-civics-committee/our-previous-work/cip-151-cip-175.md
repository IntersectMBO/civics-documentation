# CIP-151 / CIP-175

To vote in governance, a stake pool operator has to use their cold keys, the same air-gapped keys that secure the pool itself. That friction keeps many SPOs from voting at all, and low SPO turnout is a direct risk to ratifying governance actions. Two technical proposals set out to remove it, by letting SPOs vote with a hot key derived from their cold key, without ever exposing the cold infrastructure.

#### The endorsement

The Civics Committee **unanimously endorsed** [**CIP-151**](https://cips.cardano.org/cip/CIP-0151) on 16 July 2026, and sent a formal [endorsement statement](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1dMQMNP_sM1bsCZ-VkbUn3mmZK9t1iTkgUcPAataN-4E/edit?usp=sharing) to the Technical Steering Committee, asking that it be brought into scope for the next protocol hard fork.

#### The transition to CIP-175

A week later, on 23 July 2026, the committee noted that [**CIP-175**](https://cips.cardano.org/cip/CIP-0175) had emerged as the better technical path to the same goal. It moved its endorsement to CIP-175, keeping the underlying objective unchanged: let SPOs participate in governance without cold-key friction. The committee notified the TSC of the transition (30 July 2026).

This is the committee acting in its advisory role: it does not decide protocol changes, but it evaluates them, backs the ones that serve governance participation, and says so on the record.
