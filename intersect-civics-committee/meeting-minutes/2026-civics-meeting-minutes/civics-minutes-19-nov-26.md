| 2. 2027 budget process and objectives     | Lorenzo Bruno presented the 2027 budget process, following a proposal submitted with the Cardano Foundation. An NCL info action is planned for December to split the NCL into domains based on the 2027 objectives, with the NCL expiry targeted for 9 January. Objectives are framed as achievement or progress oriented; pillars represent 2030 strategy sections and domains define recommended funding groups. CIP-108 introduces categories for treasury withdrawals to improve tracking. | Thomas recommended adding a safeguard sentence so live governance actions remain unaffected by the NCL. Participants strongly recommended keeping each funding proposal to a single domain to prevent confusion.                                                                                                       |
| 3. Constitutional Committee election      | Thomas shared the CC election proposal with stricter audit rules for quality assurance. SPO participation in the election phase remains open pending a reliable tally mechanism. Voting platform options include Hydra voting on Ekklesia, CIP-179, or other open alternatives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | The committee approved the proposed timeline, starting with preparation through early April.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 4. CAP portal security and administration | Thomas reported mobile UI enhancements and new guardrails that refuse empty proposals on the CAP portal. A security incident on 17 November involved unsuccessful API break-in attempts. Submissions are limited to 2 per wallet per day, and content deletion requires an editor tag followed by admin approval to prevent single-handed actions.                                                                                                                                             | Portal administrators must be Intersect staff, with additional qualified administrators allowed. The open-source portal has daily backups (maximum 1 day of data loss) and can be self-hosted on separate domains with unified discussion funnels. Jose Miguel flagged the need for an administrative succession plan. |
| 5. CAP 12 and other CAPs                  | The TSC plans to discuss parameters and submit a response this week, and the team is awaiting a detailed expert review on the reference script.
