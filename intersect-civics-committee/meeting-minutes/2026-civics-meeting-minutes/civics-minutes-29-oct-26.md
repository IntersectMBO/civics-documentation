### Decisions/Actions

#### Decisions Made

* The committee agreed to run a DRep retirement awareness campaign: weekly social media posts and an X Space around 30 November, encouraging delegators to select new DReps ahead of the Emurgo and Yoroi DRep retirements.

#### Action Items

* NC: Draft the communication strategy for the DRep retirements and new DRep selection.
* NC: Schedule an X Space around 30 November to engage the community on the DRep changes.
* NC: Update the public governance dashboard repository to incorporate the new governance tools.
* NC: Define 2027 priorities, a document of goals for the governance coordination budget.
* The Group: Submit new governance tool suggestions to the internal chat thread.
* The Group: Share institutional feedback on the 2027 ecosystem budget process proposal via the forum or DRep Talk.

DRep retirement and voting power | Emurgo and Yoroi DReps have a tentative deregistration date of 30 November, which coincides with an epoch end and immediately removes about 660 million ADA, lowering total active voting power from roughly 5.3 billion to about 4.4 billion ADA and raising remaining active DReps' relative power by about 13%. Delegator stakes are removed on acceptance of the retirement certificate.                                                                                                 | Because delegators may not redelegate in time, the committee will run an awareness campaign pointing them to tools such as DRep Talk, Coffee Pool and the Cardano Governance Alliance. Larisa took ownership of public messaging. |
| 3. Governance tooling dashboard     | The committee reviewed the governance tooling assessment thread and proposed expanding the existing GitHub Pages dashboard to list more governance tools and discussion platforms, such as X and the Cardano Forum, in order to map dependencies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Participants are to submit new tools to the public repository via pull requests.                                                                                                                                                  |
| 4. 2027 ecosystem budget process    | Intersect, the Cardano Foundation and stakeholders published a 2027 ecosystem budget process proposal that uses domains to reduce treasury withdrawal actions, improve comparability and tie funding to deliverables. It sets seven domains plus an open-contributions category (at least 20% of the NCL), with Intersect proposed as steward of the governance coordination domain alongside the CC. Rollout needs a directional info action for DReps to approve the domains and stewards. | Constitutional Amendment Proposal 3, which divides the NCL into categories, is compatible with the domain process.                                                                                                                |
