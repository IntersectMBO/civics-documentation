### Decisions/Actions

#### Decisions Made

* Individual review format for CAP 12: The committee agreed to review and comment on CAP 12 (Dijkstra) individually rather than running a committee-wide review, given its size (over 100 new parameters) and a request from IOG's ledger team for a Civics perspective.
* Wallet governance rating framework: The committee aligned on establishing a tier-based rating framework (bronze, silver, gold) for wallet governance features, rather than directly subsidising wallet development.
* Deferred, needing further discussion: a Constitutional Committee training plan and private testnet, small maintenance grants for non-revenue governance tools, and outcome-based funding contests were all discussed without a final decision.
