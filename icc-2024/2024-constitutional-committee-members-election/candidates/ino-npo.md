**With regards to the 5 measures of competency, and with regards to your contributions summarised in the previous question, please describe why people should vote for you.**\
INO NPO is a nonprofit organization born in the heart of Estonia's digital society. Our mission is to construct accessible and legally compliant building blocks, advocating for a predictable legal framework, to advance global adoption of DAOs, guiding the digital era towards a more equitable and efficient future. In pursuit of our mission, we engage in experimentation and research, developing standards and best practices. We advocate for and facilitate the implementation of INOs across various domains, ranging from collective ownership to multi-stakeholder governance.

**Why is decentralized governance important to you?**\
It inspires collective action worldwide while utilizing accessible, equitable, code-driven and coordination.

**What perspective would you bring to the committee if successful?**\
Our exploratory research examines innovations within the future-of-work, process automation and DAO landscape spanning digital governance, operations, wrappers, security and legal frameworks - extracting cross-disciplinary insights on embedding compliance, accessibility and collective priorities that fuel equitable progress.
