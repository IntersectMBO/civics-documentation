# F.A.Q.s (English)



<details>

<summary>What is CIP-1694</summary>

CIP-1694 is a Cardano Improvement proposal that proposes a revision of Cardano's on-chain governance system to support the new requirements for Voltaire. The existing specialized governance support for protocol parameter updates and MIR certificates will be deprecated, and two new fields will be added to normal transaction bodies: governance actions, votes.\
\
Read more about this here: [https://www.1694.io](https://www.1694.io/)

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>What is the Cardano Ballot?</strong></summary>

Cardano Ballot is a user-friendly, hybrid on- and off-chain polling system that helps gauge community sentiment towards significant governance topics, including CIP-1694. Designed by the Cardano Foundation in collaboration with IOG, Cardano Ballot facilitates the participation process for ada holders while ensuring on-chain verification on the Cardano blockchain.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What are the requirements for participating in the Cardano Ballot?</summary>

Since Cardano Ballot is a stake-based application, participation using the Cardano Ballot web-based application requires: \
\
(1) a browser extension wallet (software wallet) supporting CIP-30, and (2) a minimum of one Lovelace staked before Nov 21st.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>How do I stake my ada?</strong></summary>

Ada holders have several options for staking their ada. You can delegate your stake to a stake pool run by a stake pool operator (SPO), or you can decide to run your own stake pool. Cryptocurrency exchange platforms may also offer convenient methods for staking. However, it's important to note that holding your ada within a non-custodial wallet like those referenced is a prerequisite to participating in polling events actively.

Before delegating your stake to a stake pool, you must ensure that your wallet supports staking ada. Once confirmed, transfer your ada from an exchange or any other source to your eligible wallet. Then, research and select a stake pool that meets your criteria and follow your wallet’s instructions for staking your ada.&#x20;

Users interested in running their own stake pool should have sufficient experience and thorough knowledge of managing a server, network operations, interpreting documentation, and implementing best practices, and a fundamental understanding of Cardano, blockchain, wallets, and key pairs. Ada holders without such skills or desire to run a stake pool should consider delegating their stake to an existing stake pool.

<summary><strong>What is stake delegation?</strong></summary>

Delegation is the process by which ada holders delegate the stake associated with their ada to a stake pool. It allows ada holders who do not have the skills or desire to run a node to participate in the network and be rewarded in proportion to the amount of stake delegated.



</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I participate in the Cardano Ballot?</summary>

The Cardano ballot event leverages the Cardano Foundation's innovative open-source Wallet Connector, which enables users to connect their Cardano wallet and sign their ballots, supporting CIP-30 (App-Wallet Web Bridge) &#x20;

To participate, the following steps should be taken:

1. Navigate to the Cardano Ballot page
2. First, you must connect your wallet to the Cardano ballot web-based application to participate.
3. Once your wallet is connected, click on Get Started.
4. To check if you’ve already participated, please confirm with your wallet signature.
5. Choose your preferred option: Yes/No/Abstain (Don't miss the opportunity to share any additional comments that explain your decision).
6. To finalize your decision, select 'Submit your ballot'.
7. Click on the ballot receipt once you've submitted your choice.
8. Check back on 16th December to see the results!



</details>

<details>

<summary>What question will I be asked?</summary>

As development work on the codebase continues to advance, understanding broader community sentiment on CIP-1694 minimal viable on-chain governance is vital to the next stage of the journey - a hard fork event (seamlessly, via the [hard fork combinator](https://docs.cardano.org/learn/about-hard-forks/) and SPO approval)  in the first half of 2024. The ballot will pose the question:

“Based on the current progress, should we as a Cardano community continue to develop and deploy minimum viable on-chain governance as described in CIP-1694, subject to the final approval of the SPOs?”

</details>

<details>

<summary>When will the Cardano Ballot event take place?</summary>

The ballot opens on December 01, 2023, and closes on December 11, 2023.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Will there be a snapshot?</summary>

A snapshot of all wallet balances will take place on November 21st (before 21:44 UTC) so ada holders should ensure they have moved ada funds to a compatible wallet by this date for the equivalent wallet power to be recognized in the ballot.



</details>

<details>

<summary>How much does it cost to participate in the Cardano Ballot?</summary>

There is no cost for participation, though you will need at least one lovelace to be able to participate in the ballot.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does my wallet require funds to use Cardano Ballot?</summary>

Yes, you need to stake a minimum of one Lovelace via a supported wallet before and including November 20th.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I have to lock my ada during the ballot process?</summary>

No, but you must ensure your funds are present within your registered wallet at the snapshot time (November 21st, before 21:44 UTC).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do I need funds in my wallet on November 21st even though the ballot isn’t open till December 1st?</summary>

Due to how the Cardano blockchain operates, it takes ten days (2 ‘epochs’) for staked ada to affect the network.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>How do I verify my wallet?</strong></summary>

Users don’t need to verify their wallet

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Is Cardano Ballot available on mobile devices?</strong></summary>

Cardano Ballot is a web-based application available on mobile devices with a mobile browser and a Flint wallet.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Can I use my hardware wallet for Cardano Ballot?</strong></summary>

Hardware wallets do not currently support CIP-30 and, therefore, cannot be used to participate in the Cardano Ballot.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use my testnet wallet for Cardano Ballot?</summary>

No. Users must use a Cardano Mainnet wallet to connect with Cardano Ballot.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is my Ballot Receipt?</summary>

The Ballot Receipt is your confirmation that your choice was successfully signed and submitted to the Cardano Ballot web-based application. Once your submission has been verified and submitted on-chain, your Ballot Receipt will update to include your Transaction ID and instructions on how to verify the transaction was submitted.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Why does it say pending on my receipt?</strong></summary>

Submitted ballots are batched into transactions in specific time-based periods; this accounts for the delay in receiving a transaction ID.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How is my ballot power calculated?</summary>

The Cardano Ballot is a stake-based system; therefore, the total power of a wallet will be determined by the amount of ada staked in that wallet at the time of the Snapshot (November 21st, before 21:44 UTC).

</details>

<details>

<summary>My ballot power does not match the balance of my wallet; why?</summary>

Your ballot power matches the balance of your wallet at the time of the Snapshot. If you moved your balance to a different wallet after the Snapshot, it will not match the amount.

If you add funds to your wallet after the Snapshot, that ballot power will not be considered.

</details>

You can view the ballot results once the ballot period closes on Dec 16th. You only need to visit the Cardano Ballot Home page and click on the leaderboard page to see the ballot results.\
