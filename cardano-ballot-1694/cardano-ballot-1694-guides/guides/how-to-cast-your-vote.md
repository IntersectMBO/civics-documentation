# How to cast your vote?

The Cardano ballot event leverages the Cardano Foundation's innovative open-source Wallet Connector, which enables users to connect their Cardano wallet and sign their ballots, supporting CIP-30 (App-Wallet Web Bridge)\
\
The following steps should be taken to participate:

1. Navigate to the [_Cardano Ballot page_](https://1694ballot.cardano.org)
2. To participate, first, you will need to _connect your wallet_ to the Cardano ballot web-based application.
3. Once your wallet is connected, click on _Get Started_.
4. To check if you’ve already participated, please confirm with your wallet signature.
5. Choose your preferred option: _Yes/No/Abstain_ (Don't miss the opportunity to share any additional comments that explain your decision).
6. To finalize your decision, select '_Submit your ballot_'.
7. Click on the _ballot receipt_ once you've submitted your choice.
8. Check back on 16th December to see the results!
