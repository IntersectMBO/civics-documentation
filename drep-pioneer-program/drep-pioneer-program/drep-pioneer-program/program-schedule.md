# Program Schedule

6 hours of lectures and practicals to test and apply the understanding of the concepts and ideas introduced in lectures, accompanied by video examples, guest speakers and other written material, followed by QA-sessions. Lectures will be delivered by a list of Intersect members as **Pioneer Workshop Leaders .**

DReps will be able to discuss the course and ask (and answer) questions on Discord forums, and we will try to keep the course as interactive as possible by responding to frequent questions and suggestions.

We will also publish walk-through videos for practical exercise related to how to become a DRep by using SanchoNet.

All the lectures/material will be published in a playbook format on SanchoNet. The idea of this playbook is to facilitate the DRep to follow the program and to have all the resources available in a single point.
