# Will the educational content provided by the pioneer leaders be free?

Yes! The educational content offered by the DRep workshop leaders will indeed be provided free of charge. Additionally, the program's overarching aim is to develop materials that are not only integral to the training courses but also versatile enough to be utilized independently. This means potential DReps will have access to resources that enable them to self-train and grasp the necessary concepts intuitively, facilitating a more flexible and inclusive learning experience.
