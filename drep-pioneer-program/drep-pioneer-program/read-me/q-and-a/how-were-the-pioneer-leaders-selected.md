# How were the Pioneer leaders selected?

The DRep Pioneer workshop leaders were selected through a meticulous weighted scoring method, ensuring the formation of a diverse and inclusive team that reflects a broad spectrum of regions globally. This diverse composition includes representation from Africa, Asia, Australia, Latin America, Europe, and the USA, fostering a rich tapestry of perspectives. Each chosen member brings forth their active engagement within the Cardano community and their unique backgrounds and viewpoints, enriching the collective pool of expertise and insight.
