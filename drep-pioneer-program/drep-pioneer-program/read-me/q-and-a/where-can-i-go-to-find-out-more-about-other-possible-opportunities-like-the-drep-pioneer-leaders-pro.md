# Where can I go to find out more about other possible opportunities like the DRep Pioneer Leaders pro

The DRep Pioneer Program was raised as a grant opportunity available for Intersect members. Intersect offers grants open to Intersect Members focused on achieving some aspect of Intersect's role as custodian over Cardano’s continuity and empowering and facilitating the community's ability to access Cardano’s governance features. You can find out more about [Intersects open grants here](https://docs.intersectmbo.org/intersect-community-grants/open-grants).\
