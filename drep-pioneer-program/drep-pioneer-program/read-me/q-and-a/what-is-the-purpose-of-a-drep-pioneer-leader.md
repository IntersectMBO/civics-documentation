# What is the purpose of a DRep Pioneer Leader?

A DRep Workshop Leader's role encompasses creating, curating, and refining training program content and materials. They also deliver the DRep training within their respective community or region, crafting educational content and ensuring its effective dissemination and implementation within their specific context. Essentially, they serve as ambassadors of knowledge, guiding their community members through the intricacies of DRep training, thereby fostering greater understanding and engagement within their local spheres.
