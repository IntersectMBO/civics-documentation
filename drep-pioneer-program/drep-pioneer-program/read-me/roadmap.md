# Roadmap

## **09th May - 22nd May > Phase 1**

The selected applicants of the DRep Pioneer Program become the Workshop Leaders. During Phase 1, these leaders will focus on completing and thoroughly reviewing the program content and materials.

## **23rd May - 12th July >Phase 2**

During Phase 2 the Pioneer Workshop Leaders will be accountable for delivering the DRep Pioneer Program within their respective region or community.
